In a world where shadows wake up and start killing their owners.
People start living underground to hide from the sun.

After 20 yeas in the shadows, people have learn how to avoid the sun. Stay to long on direct sunlight and could mean certain death

In this story we follow the protagonist, he is one of the few people that actually is able to live with his shadow, and cooperate. With his shadow help he can defend against other shadows, try to survive, and find out what happened 20 years ago.
